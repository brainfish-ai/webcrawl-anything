Our API Pricing

Pay per usage pricing model means you buy a credit balance and spend it on live requests. Successful calls draw from that balance. Cached, errored, and failed searches are not billed. Packages stay available after checkout, so a quiet month does not wipe the credits you already paid for. Choose a pack that matches your traffic, then top up when the balance runs low. There is no seat fee and no annual contract required to start sending requests from a staging key.
