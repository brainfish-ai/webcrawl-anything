# Creator seeding (15–20 people)

REA's spread came from third-party demos (YouTube builds, X hacks, Reddit threads), not the author's posts. Our job is to make a great demo take 10 minutes.

## Who to pick

Score each candidate 0–2 on each line and keep anyone at 6 or more.

| Signal | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posts coding-agent content (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, MCP) | weekly | monthly | rarely |
| Shows real builds on screen | always | sometimes | talking head |
| Audience | 10k–300k, engaged | 2k–10k | below 2k, or a huge account that ignores DMs |
| Has covered a dev tool launch in the last 90 days | yes | — | no |

Mix: about 8 YouTube or long-form builders, 6 X builders who post clips, 3 Reddit or newsletter writers, and 2 non-English channels (JP or KR, both big for Claude Code).

## The offer

- Hosted credits (amount to confirm) so they can show the cloud tier too. The local mode is free regardless.
- A **demo pack**: the 7 prompts in `docs/demos.md`, the clip SVGs and one "hard" prompt picked for their niche.
- A heads-up on the launch date so they can publish D+1 → D+7, with **no embargo pressure and no script**.
- Credit: featured in the README "Built with webcrawl" section and quote-tweeted from the project account.
